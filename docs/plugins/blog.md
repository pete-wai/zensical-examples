# blog

Publish posts with archives and categories. Native support was added in Zensical **0.0.64**.

Maintain a release journal; organize field reports by subject and date.

## Try it

[Open the blog example](/zensical-examples/variants/blog/).

### Post and excerpt

Open the blog to see a dated post and the excerpt before the `more` marker.

### Archive and category

Use the archive or Field notes category link to find the same post.
