## Field kit

A waterproof notebook is useful in wet weather. Moonflower opens at night.

## Filters and exclusions

Search for **field guide**, then use the tag filters to narrow results to Botany or Equipment. [Plants after dusk](plants.md) and [Equipment for dusk](equipment.md) have different tags.

The [unindexed page](unindexed.md) remains accessible through its link, but its front matter excludes it from search.
